Pass The Buck Fundraiser

The Need

Many families are on the edge of being homeless, having to choose between food or rent. Children are suffering the most. Parents are overwhelmed. We need to come beside them in a loving, long-term relationship.

The Wilmot Christian Men's Group wants to make sure no one is left behind. It's going to get worse — let's start now. Join us in this important fundraiser.

How It Works

For every $12 given, a family receives $1 a month for a minimum of a year — a regular, dependable gift they can count on.

Pass The Buck is launching with our 12 families first. Each month, we meet with them to find out what they actually need — groceries, a bill, gas, something for the kids — and make sure it's covered. Down the road, we expect to add hard-working missionaries who need to raise funds.

Donors give in $12 units and can choose which family they want to sponsor.

Why Monthly, Not All at Once

A big check spent all at once helps for a moment. A promise that shows up every month builds something more — stability, trust, and a relationship. Families know it's coming, can plan around it, and know we're walking with them, not just handing something over and walking away.

Maximum Monthly Amount

Cap: $250 per family, per month (250 × $12 sponsorships)

This cap exists so that giving spreads to help more families rather than concentrating in one place.

Accountability

  • Funds are held in a dedicated bank account set aside only for this program
  • A monthly report shows exactly where the money goes
  • 100% goes to the families in the program — no overhead
  • Gifts are accepted only by e-transfer or through GoFundMe, so every gift is documented and verifiable

Questions & Answers

How are funds distributed to families?

Funds are distributed the way the program is set up — the monthly amount a family has been sponsored for. If a family needs more than that in a given month, that's handled as a separate request, outside the regular monthly distribution.

How is the money used once a family receives it?

Funds must be used as agreed upon between the director and the family receiving support.

How often does WCMG stay in touch with families?

The director contacts each family monthly. As more donations come in for that family, the additional amount is added to what they receive.

What happens if a family still has funds left at the end of the year?

The program continues for that family, and more donations can still be accepted on their behalf.

Who makes sure funds keep moving if something happens to the director?

A backup to the director is established to make sure all funds are properly distributed even if the director is unavailable.

Can a family be removed from the program?

Yes. The director may restrict or discontinue support to a family if false information was provided, or if funds were misused.

Is there a limit to how many families the program supports?

Yes — at the director's discretion, the program can be restricted to a set number of families at any given time.

Can the program be discontinued?

Yes. Once all funds have been distributed, the director may choose to discontinue the program for any reason.

Why only e-transfer or GoFundMe, and not cash or cheque?

E-transfer and GoFundMe are the only ways we accept gifts, so that every transaction is documented and verifiable, protecting both donors and recipients.

Can funds go to someone other than the registered family member?

Only if a major incident occurs and the director agrees to the change. Otherwise, funds go only to the person registered in the program.
